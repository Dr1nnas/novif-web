Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy covers the TaskThread Export browser extension and this website, operated by novif (contact: shop@novif.app).

Summary

All processing happens locally in your browser. Your tasks, comments and the names in them are fetched from Asana's official API directly into your browser and written to a file on your device. We never receive, store, sell or share that data.

Data the extension handles

  • Task and comment data (task names, notes, comment text, author names, dates, assignees, tags). Comments can contain personal data. It is held in memory only while an export runs, then saved to the file you choose. A PDF report is kept briefly in the browser's session storage so the print tab can show it, and is cleared when the browser closes.
  • Access token. Your Asana OAuth token or Personal Access Token is stored in the extension's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device, used only to call app.asana.com, and removed when you click Disconnect or uninstall.
  • Settings and license key (plan, license key), stored locally.

Our servers

When you sign in with OAuth, the extension sends the one-time authorization code (and later a refresh token) to our small token-exchange service, which adds our app secret, forwards it to Asana and returns the token. It does not log or store tokens and never sees task data. With a Personal Access Token, no server of ours is involved.

Payments

When Pro launches, payments will be handled by a third-party payment provider, which will receive the details you give it to buy (such as email and payment details) under its own privacy policy. We will only receive whether your license is valid.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking or ads in the extension.
  • No selling or transfer of user data to third parties, and no use of it for anything other than making your export.
  • No use of the data for credit or lending decisions, and no human reads it.

The extension's use of information follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security (see GitHub's privacy statement). We use no cookies or analytics.

Your rights

Because we hold no task data, there is nothing for us to delete or export. Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored. For any privacy question or a GDPR request, email shop@novif.app.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
