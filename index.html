FAQ

Do I need to already have a domain?

No. We can help you register one (domain cost is paid separately, usually $10–20/year) or connect one you already own.

What does hosting cost?

Setup is included. Simple static sites can often be hosted for free; the care plan covers managed hosting if you prefer not to think about it.

Can I edit the site myself later?

Yes. You own all files. Or let the care plan handle edits for you.

What do you need from me?

Your business details, logo (if you have one), a few photos and your prices or menu. We guide you through it.

How do I pay?

Typically 50% to start and 50% at launch. Care plans are billed monthly.

Are the demo businesses real?

No — the barbershop, café and trades sites are fictional examples to show the style and quality you can expect.
